# Palmeraie Royal Villas — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Palmeraie Royal Villas is a 14 pool villas in a five-star resort (6,200 m²) in Palmeraie, Marrakech, Morocco. Fourteen private-pool villas among the palms. The asset is valued at €26,389,000 and divided into 263,890 shares of €100. 40% of the shares (105,556) are offered to investors; the owner retains 158,334 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 9.86% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 7.2%, i.e. a net operating income of €1,900,000 per year, distributed monthly (€158,333 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 72%. Operator / tenant: Resort management company.

## Highlights
- Eight hectares of gardens
- Golf and spa partnership

## Key risks
- Seasonality: July and August are weak
- Water costs are rising
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.
