# Atlas Olive & Wine Estate — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Atlas Olive & Wine Estate is a 28 hectares: olive groves, vines, guest house (280,000 m²) in Ourika Valley, Marrakech, Morocco. Olive groves and vines at the foot of the Atlas. The asset is valued at €3,500,000 and divided into 70,000 shares of €50. 60% of the shares (42,000) are offered to investors; the owner retains 28,000 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 8.22% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 6.0%, i.e. a net operating income of €210,000 per year, distributed monthly (€17,500 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 100%. Operator / tenant: Agricultural cooperative (long lease).

## Highlights
- 4,800 olive trees and 6 hectares of vines
- Guest house with eight rooms

## Key risks
- Harvests vary with rainfall
- Agricultural income is seasonal
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.

## Location
Ourika Valley (Marrakech). Coordinates 31.3550, -7.7690.
