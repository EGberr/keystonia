## Summary
Saint-Honoré Flagship is a two-level luxury boutique (420 m²) in Rue Saint-Honoré, Paris, France. A couture boutique on Rue Saint-Honoré. The asset is valued at €29,487,500 and divided into 117,950 shares of €250. 45% of the shares (53,077) are offered to investors; the owner retains 64,873 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 5.34% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 3.9%, i.e. a net operating income of €1,150,000 per year, distributed monthly (€95,833 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 100%. Operator / tenant: Couture maison (lease 3-6-9).

## Highlights
