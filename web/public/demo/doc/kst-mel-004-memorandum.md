# Mornington Coastal Retreat — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Mornington Coastal Retreat is a 18 villas, hot springs spa (3,900 m²) in Mornington Peninsula, Melbourne, Australia. Eighteen villas between the vines and the ocean. The asset is valued at €14,667,000 and divided into 146,670 shares of €100. 50% of the shares (73,335) are offered to investors; the owner retains 73,335 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 8.22% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 6.0%, i.e. a net operating income of €880,000 per year, distributed monthly (€73,333 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 75%. Operator / tenant: Wellness resort operator.

## Highlights
- Geothermal hot-springs spa
- Weekend occupancy above 95%

## Key risks
- Midweek demand is weaker
- Coastal planning restrictions
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.
