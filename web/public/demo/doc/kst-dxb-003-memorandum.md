# Marina Yacht Club Hotel — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Marina Yacht Club Hotel is a 80-key five-star hotel with 12 berths (9,800 m²) in Dubai Marina, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. A five-star hotel with its own yacht berths. The asset is valued at €50,000,000 and divided into 200,000 shares of €250. 35% of the shares (70,000) are offered to investors; the owner retains 130,000 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 9.32% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 6.8%, i.e. a net operating income of €3,400,000 per year, distributed monthly (€283,333 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 81%. Operator / tenant: International five-star brand (franchise).

## Highlights
- 81% occupancy, 12 superyacht berths
- Three restaurants and a beach club

## Key risks
- Hotel supply in Dubai keeps growing
- Brand franchise fees reduce margins
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.
