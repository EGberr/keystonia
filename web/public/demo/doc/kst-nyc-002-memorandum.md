# The Mercer Row Hotel — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
The Mercer Row Hotel is a 64-key five-star hotel, rooftop bar (5,200 m²) in SoHo, New York, United States. A cast-iron landmark turned five-star hotel in SoHo. The asset is valued at €59,615,000 and divided into 238,460 shares of €250. 35% of the shares (83,461) are offered to investors; the owner retains 154,999 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 7.12% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 5.2%, i.e. a net operating income of €3,100,000 per year, distributed monthly (€258,333 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 84%. Operator / tenant: Luxury hotel operator (management contract).

## Highlights
- 84% occupancy at a USD 720 average daily rate
- Rooftop bar contributes 18% of revenue
- Landmarked cast-iron facade restored in 2021

## Key risks
- Hotel income moves with tourism and business travel
- Unionised labour costs weigh on margins
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.
