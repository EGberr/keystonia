# Tribeca Sky Penthouse — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Tribeca Sky Penthouse is a 4 bedrooms, double-height living room, terrace (390 m²) in Tribeca, New York, United States. A full-floor penthouse above the Hudson. The asset is valued at €8,580,000 and divided into 85,800 shares of €100. 45% of the shares (38,610) are offered to investors; the owner retains 47,190 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 3.4% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 2.48%, i.e. a net operating income of €212,956 per year, distributed monthly (€17,746 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 100%. Operator / tenant: Corporate tenant (3-year lease).

## Highlights
- Full-floor layout with a 90 m² wrap-around terrace
- Doorman building with private lift landing
- Leased to a corporate tenant until 2028

## Key risks
- Single tenant: a departure would pause distributions
- U.S. property taxes and common charges are high and can rise
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.
