# Riad Dar Zellij — Legal structure

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Ownership
The asset is held by a dedicated special-purpose vehicle (SPV) registered in Morocco. Each Keystonia share token represents one share of that SPV. Token holders receive income pro rata and vote on major decisions (works, refinancing, sale, change of operator) through on-chain proposals with a 20% quorum.

## Transfers and compliance
Shares can only be held by investors who completed identity verification (KYC). Transfers are checked on-chain against the identity registry; jurisdiction restrictions may apply. An agent can freeze a wallet or recover shares from a lost wallet following a legal procedure.

## Exit
A sale of the asset requires a shareholder vote. Proceeds are distributed to holders pro rata to their shares.
