# Riad Dar Zellij — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Riad Dar Zellij is a 12-suite five-star riad, patio pool, hammam (1,100 m²) in Medina, Marrakech, Morocco. Twelve suites around a zellige patio and pool. The asset is valued at €7,428,500 and divided into 148,570 shares of €50. 45% of the shares (66,856) are offered to investors; the owner retains 81,714 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 9.59% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 7.0%, i.e. a net operating income of €520,000 per year, distributed monthly (€43,333 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 78%. Operator / tenant: Riad operator (management contract).

## Highlights
- Rated five stars, 9.5 guest score
- Rooftop terrace facing the Atlas
- Traditional hammam and spa

## Key risks
- Tourism-dependent income
- Dirham revenues, distributions in euro
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.
