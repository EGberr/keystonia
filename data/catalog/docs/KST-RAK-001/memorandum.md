# Riad Dar Zellij — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Riad Dar Zellij is a 7-suite riad (420 m²) in Medina, Marrakech, Morocco. Seven suites around a zellige courtyard and plunge pool. The asset is valued at €838,000 and divided into 16,760 shares of €50. 50% of the shares (8,380) are offered to investors; the owner retains 8,380 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 8.1% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 7.4%, i.e. a net operating income of €62,000 per year, distributed monthly (€5,167 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 78%. Operator / tenant: Guesthouse operator (management contract).

## Highlights
- Rooftop terrace with Atlas views
- Rated 9.4 by guests

## Key risks
- Tourism-dependent income
- Currency risk: revenue in dirham, distributions in euro
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.

## Location
Medina (Marrakech). Coordinates 31.6310, -7.9890.
