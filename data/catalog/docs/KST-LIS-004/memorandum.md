# LX Workspace — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
LX Workspace is a coworking campus (1,100 m²) in Alcântara, Lisbon, Portugal. A converted industrial hall turned coworking campus. The asset is valued at €5,500,000 and divided into 55,000 shares of €100. 50% of the shares (27,500) are offered to investors; the owner retains 27,500 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 8.22% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 6.0%, i.e. a net operating income of €330,000 per year, distributed monthly (€27,500 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 88%. Operator / tenant: Coworking operator (revenue share).

## Highlights
- Tech-startup tenant base
- Revenue-share lease with a minimum rent

## Key risks
- Coworking revenue is more volatile than a fixed lease
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.

## Location
Alcântara (Lisbon). Coordinates 38.7030, -9.1780.
