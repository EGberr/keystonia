# DIFC Dining Pavilion — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
DIFC Dining Pavilion is a three fine-dining venues (1,350 m²) in DIFC, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Three fine-dining venues in the financial district. The asset is valued at €15,000,000 and divided into 150,000 shares of €100. 45% of the shares (67,500) are offered to investors; the owner retains 82,500 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 8.22% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 6.0%, i.e. a net operating income of €900,000 per year, distributed monthly (€75,000 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 100%. Operator / tenant: Three restaurant groups (10-year leases).

## Highlights
- Base rent plus a share of turnover
- Fully let on 10-year leases

## Key risks
- Restaurant tenants fail more often than retailers
- Turnover rent varies with the season
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.

## Location
DIFC (Dubai). Coordinates 25.2131, 55.2796.
