# Ourika Olive Grove — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Ourika Olive Grove is a 12 hectares, 3,400 olive trees (120,000 m²) in Ourika Valley, Marrakech, Morocco. 3,400 olive trees at the foot of the Atlas. The asset is valued at €350,000 and divided into 7,000 shares of €50. 60% of the shares (4,200) are offered to investors; the owner retains 2,800 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 5.4% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 6.0%, i.e. a net operating income of €21,000 per year, distributed monthly (€1,750 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 100%. Operator / tenant: Agricultural cooperative (harvest contract).

## Highlights
- Organic certification since 2021
- Oil sold to a regional mill under contract

## Key risks
- Harvest depends on rainfall
- Agricultural land rules for foreign owners (held via local SPV)
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.

## Location
Ourika Valley (Marrakech). Coordinates 31.3550, -7.7690.
