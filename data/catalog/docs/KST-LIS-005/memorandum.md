# Comporta Vineyard Estate — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Comporta Vineyard Estate is a 18 hectares of vines (180,000 m²) in Comporta, Lisbon, Portugal. Eighteen hectares of vines on the Alentejo coast. The asset is valued at €1,040,000 and divided into 20,800 shares of €50. 55% of the shares (11,440) are offered to investors; the owner retains 9,360 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 4.9% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 5.0%, i.e. a net operating income of €52,000 per year, distributed monthly (€4,333 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 100%. Operator / tenant: Winery (long-term land lease).

## Highlights
- Leased to an award-winning winery
- Land in a protected natural area

## Key risks
- Climate risk on harvests
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.

Comporta (Lisbon). Coordinates 38.3800, -8.7860.
