# Palais Kasbah & Spa — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Palais Kasbah & Spa is a 22-suite palace riad with spa (3,100 m²) in Kasbah, Marrakech, Morocco. A restored palace by the Saadian Tombs. The asset is valued at €17,857,000 and divided into 178,570 shares of €100. 40% of the shares (71,428) are offered to investors; the owner retains 107,142 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 9.59% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 7.0%, i.e. a net operating income of €1,250,000 per year, distributed monthly (€104,167 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 74%. Operator / tenant: Luxury hotel group (lease).

## Highlights
- Three courtyards and a 600 m² spa
- 20-year lease to a luxury group

## Key risks
- Restoration reserve needed for a historic palace
- Currency risk on dirham income
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.
