# Riad Kasbah Garden — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Riad Kasbah Garden is a 6-room riad (360 m²) in Kasbah, Marrakech, Morocco. A garden riad near the Saadian Tombs, opening 2027. The asset is valued at €640,000 and divided into 12,800 shares of €50. 50% of the shares (6,400) are offered to investors; the owner retains 6,400 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 7.9% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 7.5%, i.e. a net operating income of €48,000 per year, distributed monthly (€4,000 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 0%. Operator / tenant: Opening after renovation.

## Highlights
- Renovation financed by this offering
- Orange-tree garden patio

## Key risks
- Development risk: income starts after works
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.
