# Fifth Avenue Flagship — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Fifth Avenue Flagship is a three-level flagship store (1,100 m²) in Fifth Avenue, New York, United States. Three floors of flagship retail on the world's most expensive street. The asset is valued at €59,090,000 and divided into 236,360 shares of €250. 40% of the shares (94,544) are offered to investors; the owner retains 141,816 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 6.03% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 4.4%, i.e. a net operating income of €2,600,000 per year, distributed monthly (€216,667 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 100%. Operator / tenant: Luxury maison (15-year lease).

## Highlights
- 15-year lease with 3% annual escalations
- 12 m of frontage between 56th and 57th Street

## Key risks
- Single-tenant income
- Prime retail rents are cyclical
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.
