> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Chelsea Garden Townhouse is a three lateral apartments and a private garden (420 m²) in Chelsea, London, United Kingdom. A stucco townhouse off the King's Road. The asset is valued at €5,014,000 and divided into 50,140 shares of €100. 45% of the shares (22,563) are offered to investors; the owner retains 27,577 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 3.3% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 2.41%, i.e. a net operating income of €120,787 per year, distributed monthly (€10,066 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 100%. Operator / tenant: Assured shorthold tenancies.
