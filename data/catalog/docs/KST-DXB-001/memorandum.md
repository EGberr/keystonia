## Summary
Palm Signature Villas is a 6 beachfront villas with private pools (5,400 m²) in Palm Jumeirah, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Six beachfront villas on the Palm's outer frond. The asset is valued at €34,375,000 and divided into 137,500 shares of €250. 40% of the shares (55,000) are offered to investors; the owner retains 82,500 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 8.77% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 6.4%, i.e. a net operating income of €2,200,000 per year, distributed monthly (€183,333 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 77%. Operator / tenant: Resort management company.

## Highlights
- Private beach and pool for every villa
- Average nightly rate above AED 14,000
- Managed with hotel services and a guaranteed floor

## Key risks

- New supply on the Palm could pressure rates
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.
