# Yarra Valley Vineyard Estate — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Yarra Valley Vineyard Estate is a 42 hectares of pinot noir and chardonnay, cellar door (420,000 m²) in Yarra Valley, Melbourne, Australia. Cool-climate vines and a cellar door an hour from the city. The asset is valued at €11,636,500 and divided into 232,730 shares of €50. 55% of the shares (128,001) are offered to investors; the owner retains 104,729 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 7.53% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 5.5%, i.e. a net operating income of €640,000 per year, distributed monthly (€53,333 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 100%. Operator / tenant: Winery (long-term lease with revenue share).

## Highlights
- 42 hectares under vine
- Cellar door and restaurant with 60,000 visitors a year

## Key risks

- Wine prices move with export demand
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.
