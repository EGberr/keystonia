# Collins Street Grand Hotel — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Collins Street Grand Hotel is a 56-key five-star heritage hotel (6,100 m²) in Collins Street, Melbourne, Australia. A gold-rush era grand hotel on the Paris end of Collins Street. The asset is valued at €37,500,000 and divided into 150,000 shares of €250. 40% of the shares (60,000) are offered to investors; the owner retains 90,000 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 7.67% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 5.6%, i.e. a net operating income of €2,100,000 per year, distributed monthly (€175,000 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 82%. Operator / tenant: Heritage hotel operator (management contract).

## Highlights
- 82% occupancy, restored ballroom
- Events contribute a quarter of revenue

## Key risks
- Heritage overlay limits changes
- Business-travel dependent midweek
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.
