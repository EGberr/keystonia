# Quai d'Anjou Residence — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Quai d'Anjou Residence is a 3 bedrooms on the seine, 3.6 m ceilings (168 m²) in Île Saint-Louis, Paris, France. Seine views from a 17th-century quay. The asset is valued at €2,230,000 and divided into 22,300 shares of €100. 40% of the shares (8,920) are offered to investors; the owner retains 13,380 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 3.0% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 2.19%, i.e. a net operating income of €48,837 per year, distributed monthly (€4,070 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 100%. Operator / tenant: Long-term tenant (6-year lease).

## Highlights
- Five windows on the Seine
- Period panelling and Versailles parquet restored in 2023

## Key risks

- Flood-zone insurance premium
- Shares are illiquid outside the Keystonia secondary market; prices may differ from the valuation.
- Valuations can go down as well as up.
