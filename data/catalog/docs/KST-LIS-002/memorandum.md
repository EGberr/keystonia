# Príncipe Real Guesthouse — Investment memorandum

> **Demo document.** This asset is fictional and part of the Keystonia demonstration network. No real investment is offered.

## Summary
Príncipe Real Guesthouse is a 10-room guesthouse (520 m²) in Príncipe Real, Lisbon, Portugal. A tiled 19th-century townhouse turned guesthouse. The asset is valued at €2,586,000 and divided into 51,720 shares of €50. 45% of the shares (23,274) are offered to investors; the owner retains 28,446 shares.

## Income
Gross yield is 7.95% and the projected net yield after operating costs is 5.8%, i.e. a net operating income of €150,000 per year, distributed monthly (€12,500 per month across all shares). Occupancy is 83%. Operator / tenant: Guesthouse operator (lease).
